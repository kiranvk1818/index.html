Questions

Good to know.

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. The link opens in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop. They tap and read.

How fast do I get my link?

Within 24 hours of sending us your details. Same-day delivery is available for an extra ₹400.

What if the date or venue changes?

Send us the change. We update the page and the same link keeps working, so nobody needs a new invitation.

Which languages can the invitation use?

English, Malayalam, Arabic, Hindi and Tamil are ready. Ask us if you need another.

Is it only for weddings?

No. We make pages for nikah, muhurtham and church weddings, and also for birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, housewarmings and graduations.
